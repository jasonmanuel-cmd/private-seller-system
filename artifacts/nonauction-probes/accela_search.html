Important Security Notice: Phishing Email Alert

We have been informed that some customers who submitted a building permit application through the Accela Citizen Portal may receive a phishing email. The subject line of the phishing email is "Outstanding Processing Fee Required Prior to Final Approval" If you receive the email, we recommend updating your password in the Accela Citizen Portal, do not click on any links or download attachments, do not respond or provide any personal information, delete the email, and block the sender. For your reference, you may view a copy of the phishing email here. Your account security is important to us, and we are actively monitoring the situation.
