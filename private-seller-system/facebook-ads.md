## Lead Form vs Landing Page

**Option A: Facebook Lead Form (Higher conversion, lower quality)**
- Form asks: Address, Why private?, Timeline, Phone
- Facebook auto-fills phone → more leads, but some spam

**Option B: Landing Page Traffic (Lower conversion, higher quality) — RECOMMENDED**
- Ad → /private-sale landing page → they read and fill your Google Form or call
- Better quality, they self-select private sellers

Start with Option B.
