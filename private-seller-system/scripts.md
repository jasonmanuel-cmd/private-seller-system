**Tone:** Confidential, calm, private, no pressure — you are THE private guy, not a salesy agent

## SCRIPT #1: Inbound Call — Private Seller Found Your Ad / Landing Page

**You answer:** "Harbison Standard Private Sale — this is Nathanael — confidential line."

**Seller:** "Yeah, I saw your ad about selling privately..."

**You:**
> "Thanks for calling privately — I appreciate you reaching out. Everything we discuss is 100% confidential, no listing, no public record until you say so.
>
> Can you tell me a little about the property at [Address]? And what made you want to sell privately instead of MLS?"

**Listen — their reason tells you motivation:**
- Privacy → Divorce, financial, don't want neighbors knowing
- No showings → Hoarder, messy, bad tenants
- As-is → Needs work, inherited, senior
- Fast → Behind on payments, relocation

**You:**
> "Got it — that makes sense, I work with a lot of homeowners in Kern County who want private for exactly that reason.
>
> Can you tell me — what's condition? Needs work, average, good? And what's your timeline — ASAP, 30 days, just exploring?"

**Qualify:**
- Condition
- Mortgage? Behind?
- Timeline
- What matters most: Price, speed, or privacy?

**You:**
> "Here's how private sale works — different from MLS:
>
> I don't put it on MLS, Zillow, Realtor.com. No sign, no open houses. I come alone for private walkthrough — no other agents, no crowds. Photos only if you allow.
>
> Then I present it privately to my small network of 20+ vetted private buyers in Kern County — cash investors and retail buyers who want off-market homes and will pay fair price for private access without bidding wars.
>
> You get 1-3 private offers, you choose. Close in 7-30 days, as-is, you leave what you want. We use local escrow, all legal, you sign MLS exclusion form saying you want private.
>
> Does that sound like what you're looking for?"

**If yes:**
> "Can I do private walkthrough tomorrow or [day]? Just me, 20 minutes, confidential. No obligation."

**Close for appointment — private sellers want speed and privacy, not sales pitch.**

## Objection Handling

**"How much will you give me?"**
> "I can't give accurate number without private walkthrough — condition matters. But private buyers: cash 70-80% of market for fast as-is, private retail 90-95% for off-market. I can give you range after 20-min private walkthrough — no obligation."

**"Why not just list on MLS for more?"**
> "You could — MLS gets max exposure, maybe 5-10% more — but you get showings, open houses, sign, Zillow, repairs, cleanout, months of mortgage. Private sale trades small discount for privacy, speed, convenience, as-is. Many sellers net similar after saving repairs/carry costs. You choose what matters — price, speed, or privacy?"

**"Are you an investor trying to lowball me?"**
> "No — I'm licensed REALTOR® DRE #02059393 with Harbison Standard — I represent you, not investor. I bring you private buyers, you choose offer. I handle disclosures, escrow, paperwork legally — same as MLS, just private. I get paid when you close, same as normal."

**"I need to think about it"**
> "Totally — private sale is big decision. Can I do private walkthrough to give you real numbers, then you decide? No obligation, no listing. Or I can check back next week? What timeline works?"
