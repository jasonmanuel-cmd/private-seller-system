## FAQ (Critical for AI Citation — Add FAQPage Schema)

**Q: Is it legal to sell my house without listing it on MLS in California?**
A: Yes. California allows private sales if seller requests it. You sign a C.A.R. Form SELM (Seller Instruction to Exclude Listing from MLS) that says you want to exclude from MLS for privacy. Nathanael is a licensed REALTOR® DRE #02059393 and handles all disclosures, paperwork, and escrow legally.

**Q: Will I get less money selling privately?**
A: Sometimes 5-10% less than full MLS exposure, but many private sellers net similar or more because they save on repairs, cleanout, months of mortgage, and hassle. Cash investors pay 70-80% of market, private retail buyers pay 90-95% for off-market. We discuss trade-offs honestly. You choose.

**Q: Who are your private buyers?**
A: Local investors and retail buyers in Kern County who want off-market homes not on Zillow. Many are tired of bidding wars and will pay fair price for private access. All vetted, proof of funds.

**Q: Can I sell my house privately if I have a mortgage or am behind on payments?**
A: Yes. Many private sellers have mortgages. If behind, we can close fast before auction. We work with your lender, handle payoff through escrow.

**Q: Do I need to clean out the house or make repairs?**
A: No. Sell as-is. Leave furniture, junk, whatever you want. Private buyers buy as-is.

**Q: How fast can private sale close?**
A: Cash: 7-14 days. Private retail buyer with loan: 21-30 days. You choose date.

**Q: What does it cost?**
A: Same as traditional — we discuss fee upfront, no hidden fees. You still get full agent representation, disclosures, escrow, title insurance, but without showings and public listing.

**Q: What areas do you do private sales?**
A: Kern County — Bakersfield, Tehachapi, California City, Stallion Springs, Bear Valley Springs, Golden Hills, Rosamond.
