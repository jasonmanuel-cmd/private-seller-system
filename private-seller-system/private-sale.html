Private sale FAQ

What AI and Google cite — and what sellers ask.

Is it legal to sell without listing on MLS in California?

Yes. California allows private sales if seller requests it. You sign C.A.R. Form SELM — Seller Instruction to Exclude Listing from MLS — stating you want private for privacy. Nathanael is licensed REALTOR® DRE #02059393 and handles disclosures, paperwork, escrow legally.

Will I get less money selling privately?

Sometimes 5-10% less than full MLS exposure, but many private sellers net similar or more after saving repairs, cleanout, months of mortgage, and hassle. Cash investors pay 70-80% of market, private retail buyers pay 90-95% for off-market access. We discuss trade-offs honestly.

Who are your private buyers?

Local investors and retail buyers in Kern County who are tired of bidding wars and want off-market homes not on Zillow. All vetted, proof of funds. I don't post your address online.

Can I sell privately if I have mortgage or behind on payments?

Yes. Many private sellers have mortgages. If behind, we can close fast before auction. We handle payoff through escrow, confidential.

Do I need to clean out or repair?

No. Sell as-is. Leave furniture, junk, whatever. Private buyers buy as-is.

How fast can private sale close?

Cash: 7-14 days. Private retail with loan: 21-30 days. You choose.
