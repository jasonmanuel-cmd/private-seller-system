## PHASE 4: The Private Sale Process (What You Tell Seller)

Once they call, you need a clear private process:

**Step 1: Private Conversation (15 min phone)**
- Why private? Timeline? Condition? Mortgage? What matters most — price, speed, privacy?
- Explain: "Private sale means we don't put it on MLS, Zillow, Realtor.com. No sign, no open houses. I show it privately to my small network of vetted buyers (investors + retail buyers who want off-market). You control who sees it."

**Step 2: Private Walkthrough (You only)**
- You go alone, no other agents, no crowds. Take photos ONLY if seller allows (some private sellers don't want photos online — you can sell without photos to private network)

**Step 3: Private Buyer Network**
- You have 2 buyer types:
  - Cash investors: Close in 7-14 days, as-is, 70-80% of market value
  - Private retail buyers: Want off-market deals, will pay 90-95% of market, close 21-30 days, as-is
- You present 1-3 private offers, seller chooses

**Step 4: Close Quietly**
- Use local escrow, no public marketing until closed (if ever — some private sales never hit public record as listing, only as sold)
- Seller can leave what they want, no cleanout

**Legal in CA:** Yes, but seller must sign MLS exclusion form (C.A.R. Form SELM — Seller Instruction to Exclude Listing from MLS) + you disclose you are agent. Private sales are legal if seller requests privacy.

**Your Fee:** Same as normal (2.5-3% to you) or flat fee for private program — explain you still handle paperwork, disclosures, escrow, but save them hassle of showings.
