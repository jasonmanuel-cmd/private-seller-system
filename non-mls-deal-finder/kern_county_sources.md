# Kern County Free Public Data Sources — No MLS, $0

All these are 100% free, public record, no login required (or free login).

## 1. Kern County Assessor — Parcel Search (FREE)
**URL:** https://assessor.kerncounty.com/parcel-search/
**What you get:** Owner name, mailing address, assessed value, lot size, APN, property address
**How to use:**
- Enter address or APN
- If mailing address != property address = absentee owner (more motivated)
- If owner name = LLC or Trust = possible investor tired of property
- Use to verify any Craigslist / Zillow FSBO lead

**Free owner type detection:**
- Out-of-state: Mailing address state != CA
- High equity: Assessed value low vs market, owned 10+ years (check sale date)

## 2. Kern County Treasurer & Tax Collector — Tax-Defaulted Sales
**URL:** https://www.kcttc.co.kern.ca.us/tax-defaulted-property-sales/
**What you get:** PDF list of properties with 5+ years unpaid taxes, going to auction
**How to use:**
- Download latest "Tax-Defaulted Property List" PDF (usually updated yearly, with prior year lists)
- Columns: APN, Situs Address, Assessed Value, Minimum Bid
- Action: BEFORE auction, contact owner via TruePeopleSearch.com — offer to buy and pay taxes + cash to them. Owner can redeem up to 5pm day before auction.
- Why it's a deal: Owner hasn't paid taxes in 5 years = doesn't want property or can't afford = will sell cheap

**Example 2024 list had 300+ parcels in California City at $5k-15k minimum bid**

## 3. Kern County Recorder — Official Records Search (NOD)
**URL:** https://recorder.kerncounty.com/ > Search Official Records
**What you get:** Notice of Default (NOD), Notice of Trustee Sale (NTS), Liens
**How to use:**
- Search > Document Type: NOTICE OF DEFAULT > Date: Last 30 days
- Results: Grantor (owner), Grantee (lender), APN, Date, Document Number
- NOD means homeowner 90+ days late, lender started foreclosure — has 90 days to sell before auction
- Contact owner ASAP — offer cash, quick close, as-is

**Cost:** Search free, view index free. Full doc image $1-2 if needed (optional)

## 4. Kern County Superior Court — Probate Case Search
**URL:** https://www.kern.courts.ca.gov/ > Online Services > Case Search > Probate
**What you get:** New probate cases (someone died, estate being settled)
**How to use:**
- Search Case Type: Probate, Filed Date: Last 90 days
- Case shows Decedent name, Petitioner (usually heir/executor), Attorney
- Cross-ref decedent name with Assessor search to find properties they owned
- Contact petitioner/attorney: "I help families sell inherited homes as-is in Kern County, no cleanup needed"

**Why deal:** Heirs often live out of state, want fast cash, house may need work

## 5. City of Bakersfield Open Data — Code Enforcement
**URL:** https://data.bakersfieldcity.us/
**Search:** "Code Enforcement" or "Nuisance"
**What you get:** Addresses with open code violations: overgrown weeds, abandoned, unsafe
**How to use:**
- Download CSV of open cases
- Filter for residential, violation type: "Vacant", "Overgrown", "Substandard"
- Look up owner via Assessor, contact

## 10. Driving for Dollars (Free, Best for Vacant)
Use Kern County Assessor app on phone (mobile site works):
- Drive Golden Hills, Bear Valley Springs, Stallion Springs, California City, Oildale
- Look for: tall weeds, boarded windows, no car in driveway for months, piled mail, tarp roof
- Write address, look up owner free via Assessor on spot, check if absentee
- Door knock or mail letter: "I want to buy your house at 123 Main as-is"

## How to Skip Trace for Free (Get Phone)

1. **Assessor gives owner name + mailing address**
2. **TruePeopleSearch.com:** Enter name + city + state → free phone, relatives, emails
3. **FastPeopleSearch.com:** Same, sometimes better
4. **WhitePages.com:** Free preview gives 1 number

If free fails, paid skip is $0.15 via IDI Data or BatchLeads (optional)

## Legal Note (California)
- All data above is public record, legal to access
- Contacting owners is legal, but follow Do Not Call if they ask to stop
- If wholesaling (assigning contract), you must disclose you are not the end buyer and you are a licensed agent (DRE #02059393) — California requires disclosure
- For probate, don't contact grieving family within 30 days of death — wait, or contact attorney
